Biography

Sharing a Passion for Art

I am a Montreal based artist and art instructor who teaches in both French and English. I love painting and helping others express themselves creatively. Art has always been a big part of my life, and I enjoy sharing that passion with people of all ages and skill levels.

Inspiring Creativity Through Art

I teach a variety of mediums including watercolor, oil painting, acrylic, pastel and charcoal. Whether someone is picking up a brush for the first time or continuing to grow their skills, my goal is to make the process feel natural, enjoyable and accessible.
Through classes, workshops, creative events and live painting sessions, I aim to create a relaxed and inspiring environment where people feel comfortable exploring their creativity. For me, art is about expression, confidence and finding joy in the process.
