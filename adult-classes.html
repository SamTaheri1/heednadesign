Drawing & Painting

Adult Courses

We offer different levels from beginners to advanced. We offer online and in-person drawing and painting courses for all ages and levels.

Drawing courses: Adult art classes provide a platform for students to explore and understand perspective, lighting, and contrast through drawing. They learn to capture photos, including still life, nature, landscapes, and portraits. By developing their drawing skills and hand-eye coordination, they enhance their artistic abilities. These classes also introduce techniques like hatching, cross-hatching, and blending, using different materials for artistic expression.

Painting courses: In the art classes, students will be taught various techniques for painting different objects using oil and acrylic paints. They will learn how to mix colors, work with tonal values, and create well-balanced compositions.
